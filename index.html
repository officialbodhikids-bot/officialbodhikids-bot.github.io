Biography

KiB Tara

"KiB Tara" is a dedicated academic professional, engineer, and educator with over five years of experience teaching and mentoring students. Holding advanced degrees in Engineering and Computer Science, she is passionate about making complex ideas simple, engaging, and impactful.

Fluent in English, Tamil, and Telugu, Jegatha brings her love for storytelling and traditional Indian philosophy together to write meaningful, bilingual children's books. Through her writing, she aims to help young minds build emotional resilience, mindfulness, and a lifelong love for learning.
